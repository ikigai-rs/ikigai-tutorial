# The other books

gonk is built from the pieces [The ikigai Book](../index.html) teaches: resources named by URIs and
resolved by a kernel, spaces composed into a host, capabilities that are declared and enforced,
golden threads that say what a write invalidates, and the transports that make a kernel reachable
from another process or another machine. This book uses those ideas without re-teaching them; that
book is where they are taught, from the first endpoint up. [Building ikigai UIs](../ui/index.html) builds views as resources, and [The ikigai
Architecture](../architecture/index.html) carries a ledger item, this book's own subject, through
the whole substrate. [Running ikigai](../operations/index.html) is the operator's book for the
`ikigai` binary this book's QUIC client chapters use.

[The library](../library/index.html) lists all five, with who each is for, what to read first and
where to start.

The books are built, tested and published together, from one repository:
<https://github.com/ikigai-rs/ikigai-tutorial>.
