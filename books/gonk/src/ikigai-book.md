# The ikigai Book

gonk is built from the pieces [The ikigai Book](../index.html) teaches: resources named by
URIs and resolved by a kernel, spaces composed into a host, capabilities that are declared and
enforced, golden threads that say what a write invalidates, and the transports that make a
kernel reachable from another process or another machine. This book uses those ideas without
re-teaching them; that book is where they are taught, from the first endpoint up.

The two books are built, tested and published together, from one repository:
<https://github.com/ikigai-rs/ikigai-tutorial>.
