# The other books

This book is about running the `ikigai` binary. What it runs is taught elsewhere:
[The ikigai Book](../index.html) teaches resources, spaces, capabilities and golden threads from the
first endpoint up, and it is where to go when a chapter here uses one of those words.
[The gonk Book](../gonk/index.html) covers gonk, the work ledger and review host composed from the
same pieces, with its own doors and QUIC client; [Building ikigai UIs](../ui/index.html) builds
views as resources; and [The ikigai Architecture](../architecture/index.html) shows how the pieces
fit.

[The library](../library/index.html) lists all five, with who each is for, what to read first and
where to start.

The books are built, tested and published together, from one repository:
<https://github.com/ikigai-rs/ikigai-tutorial>.
