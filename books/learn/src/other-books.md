# The other books

This book is about teaching with ikigai. What it teaches with is taught elsewhere:
[The ikigai Book](../index.html) teaches resources, spaces, capabilities and golden threads from the
first endpoint up, and it is where to go when a chapter here uses one of those words.
[The gonk Book](../gonk/index.html) covers gonk and the work ledger, which is where a learner's
work is kept; [Building ikigai UIs](../ui/index.html) builds views as resources; [The ikigai
Architecture](../architecture/index.html) shows how the pieces fit; and [Running
ikigai](../operations/index.html) is the operator's book for the `ikigai` binary.

[The library](../library/index.html) lists all six, with who each is for, what to read first and
where to start.

The books are built, tested and published together, from one repository:
<https://github.com/ikigai-rs/ikigai-tutorial>.
